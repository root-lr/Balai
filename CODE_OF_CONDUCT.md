# Code of conduct

Balai follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- **Be kind and respectful.** Everyone was a beginner once.
- **Be constructive.** Criticize ideas and code, never people.
- **Be inclusive.** No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind, in any language.
- **Assume good faith**, and ask when something is unclear.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/root-lr/Balai/security/advisories/new) or by message to [@root-lr](https://github.com/root-lr). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that don't follow this code, and may ban contributors who repeatedly ignore it.
